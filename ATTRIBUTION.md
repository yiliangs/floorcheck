# Attribution

## The Modified Swiss Dwellings documents

The plan documents under `data/msd/` are derived from the Modified Swiss Dwellings (MSD)
dataset, which is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They are redistributed here under
the same licence.

Source: Casper van Engelenburg, Fatemeh Mostafavi, Emanuel Kuhn, Yuntae Jeon, Michael Franzen,
Matthias Standfest, Jan van Gemert and Seyran Khademi. "MSD: A Benchmark Dataset for Floor Plan
Generation of Building Complexes." European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2024.
doi:10.1007/978-3-031-73636-0_4. Dataset: https://data.4tu.nl/datasets/e1d89cb5-6872-48fc-be63-aadd687ee6f9

Changes made: each document restates one floor (`data/msd/msd/`) or one apartment of a floor
(`data/msd/msd_units/`) of the first 4,167 floors of the training split, in sorted identifier
order, in floorcheck's plan schema. `floorcheck-export` in this archive wrote them from the
extracted training split and reproduces them byte for byte; no document is edited by hand.

The CC BY 4.0 licence applies to these documents only. The software in this archive is
licensed under the terms in `LICENSE`.

## The generated plans

The plan documents under `data/generated/` were generated by the author of this archive, who ran
each generator's released code and weights and converted every sample with `floorcheck-export`.
Their geometry is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
licence (CC BY-NC 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/), chosen to respect the
generators' non-commercial terms: the House-GAN and House-GAN++ code headers restrict use to
research, House-GAN's weights carry a non-commercial licence, and HouseDiffusion's code and
weights are not for commercial use. The CC BY-NC 4.0 licence applies to these documents only.
The generators are:

- House-GAN (`housegan/`): Nelson Nauata, Kai-Hung Chang, Chin-Yi Cheng, Greg Mori and Yasutaka
  Furukawa. "House-GAN: Relational Generative Adversarial Networks for Graph-Constrained House
  Layout Generation." European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2020.
  doi:10.1007/978-3-030-58452-8_10. Code: https://github.com/ennauata/housegan, GNU GPL v3 with a
  research-use-only header; its weights carry a non-commercial research licence.
- House-GAN++ (`houseganpp/`): Nelson Nauata, Sepidehsadat Hosseini, Kai-Hung Chang, Hang Chu,
  Chin-Yi Cheng and Yasutaka Furukawa. "House-GAN++: Generative Adversarial Layout Refinement
  Network towards Intelligent Computational Agent for Professional Architects." IEEE/CVF
  Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2021.
  doi:10.1109/CVPR46437.2021.01342. Code: https://github.com/ennauata/houseganpp, GNU GPL v3 with a
  research-use-only header.
- HouseDiffusion (`housediffusion/`): Mohammad Amin Shabani, Sepidehsadat Hosseini and Yasutaka
  Furukawa. "HouseDiffusion: Vector Floorplan Generation via a Diffusion Model with Discrete and
  Continuous Denoising." IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR),
  2023. arXiv:2211.13287. Code: https://github.com/aminshabani/house_diffusion, GNU GPL v3 for
  research use; its code and weights are not for commercial use.
- GSDiff (`gsdiff/`): Sizhe Hu, Wenming Wu, Yuntao Wang, Benzhu Xu and Liping Zheng. "GSDiff:
  Synthesizing Vector Floorplans via Geometry-Enhanced Structural Graph Generation." Proceedings
  of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence 39(16), 2025. doi:10.1609/aaai.v39i16.33904.
  Code: https://github.com/SizheHu/GSDiff, GNU GPL v3.

The room programs the generators were conditioned on are drawn from RPLAN (Wenming Wu, Xiao-Ming
Fu, Rui Tang, Yuhan Wang, Yu-Hao Qi and Ligang Liu. "Data-driven Interior Plan Generation for
Residential Buildings." ACM Transactions on Graphics 38(6), 2019, doi:10.1145/3355089.3356556), whose licence forbids
redistribution. Of that conditioning only each generated room's type is kept. The RPLAN plan
identifiers, record indices and buckets, and the requested adjacency graphs, doors and entries,
are withheld, and each document is named `<generator>-<NNNN>` in the order of a hash of its own
content.
